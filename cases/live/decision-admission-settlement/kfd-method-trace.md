# KFD Method Trace

## KFD-1: preserve genesis and qualification separately

The record keeps the maintainer's report of the unretained conversation,
the pre-discovery public ontology, the formalizing commit's `Agent: Codex`
declaration, the human authorization, and the independent review as different
facts. Later acceptance does not rewrite who supplied the candidate.

## KFD-2: bound every claim to its evidence

The authorship claim rests on maintainer testimony plus the public
`Agent: Codex` formalization coordinate. The semantic claim rests on the prior
KFD-2/KFD-7/KFD-10 contracts, the KFD-11 schema, deletion and fuse witnesses,
and repository review. Neither evidence class proves the other.

## KFD-3: preserve distinct participant value

The human supplied the question, reality pressure, authorization, and final
value judgment. The agent supplied the missing Admission responsibility and
the structural compression. Independent review supplied a separate challenge
and verification boundary. Treating any one participant as the sole source
would erase the collaboration that made the result possible.

## KFD-4: change the consequence-bearing observer

```text
numbered-standard editor
  asks which visible nouns deserve KFD numbers

state-admission authority
  asks what must remain distinct before a successor Fact may exist
```

The first view naturally saw Claim, Assessment, and Decision. The transformed
view saw that the requested effect still had to cross an owning authority and
could fail independently. Admission became visible, and the unit of design
changed from three candidate nouns to one consequential settlement procedure.

## KFD-5: qualify the generated procedure

Qualification did not rely on origin prestige. It tested whether deleting or
fusing a role changes consequential decisions, compared alternative
allocations, published the contract and schema, retained falsifiers, and
required independent review.

The current outcome is **provisional**. The procedure is strong enough to hold
a numbered draft and guide implementations, but KFD has not yet established
independent cross-domain adoption, universal minimality, acceptable cost, or
the activation gate.

## Method boundary

This case shows that an agent can generate a Primitive candidate by changing
perspective and compressing existing responsibilities. It does not show that
perspective transformation is always the best discovery method, that every
Agent proposal should be promoted, or that human judgment becomes optional.
