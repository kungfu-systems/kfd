# Responsibility Split

```text
KFD-2 trust world
  Claim -> Assessment

KFD-10 authority geometry
  Warrant -> Decision authority

KFD-7 Fact-Episode Ontology
  requested effect -> Admission -> successor Fact
```

KFD-11 does not create a second Claim, Assessment, Warrant, Fact, or Episode
authority. It names the procedure that carries one consequential proposition
from purpose-bound trust judgment through authorized disposition to the
independently recorded result of applying that disposition.

Domain Profiles own proposition kinds, assessment methods, disposition
vocabulary, transaction technology, retry policy, and participant projection.
They may not redefine a passing Assessment as authority or a valid Decision as
successful Admission.
