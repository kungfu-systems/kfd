# Propagation Hypothesis

Agent work crosses repositories, tools, organizations, and authority systems.
At each boundary, persuasive output is cheap but admitted consequence is not.
The KFD-11 procedure may propagate because it lets downstream participants ask
four stable questions without reconstructing the producer's context:

```text
What is claimed?
For what purpose was it assessed?
Who authorized which disposition?
What effect was actually admitted?
```

Release passports, review decisions, project cuts, mandates, regulated
submissions, and other proof-carrying work objects may implement this procedure.
This is a hypothesis, not evidence of adoption or superiority.
