# Distinguishability and redundancy

## Minimum closure

The useful recursive path needs an exact predecessor, a candidate genesis, a
fact-bound assessment, a verifier independent of the generator, a review role
independent of the author, a disposition authority, and explicit admission or
retained non-promotion.

## Deletion test

Delete the Candidate while retaining KFD-1, KFD-2, KFD-5, KFD-11, and the
Self-Conformance Profile. Every decision-relevant observation remains
expressible: roots cannot drift; claims remain assessable; genesis remains
separate from qualification; decision remains separate from admission; and the
Profile retains candidate, qualification, rejection, provisional, and
`no-new-kfd` transitions.

The deletion therefore removes a convenient name but does not yet remove an
independent responsibility.

## Fuse test

Fuse the Candidate into KFD-5 as “apply this procedure to KFD itself.” The
fixed-root and trust duties remain supplied by KFD-1 and KFD-2, while KFD-11
supplies the authority-separated terminal path. No contradiction or hidden
authority appears. Fuse into the Profile instead and the normative claim
becomes implementation-specific, so the Profile cannot be the sole authority.

## Cross-domain test

The same composition is plausible outside KFD but no independent adopter cut
is retained. This is a gap for a new universal procedure, but not a gap in the
claim that KFD can dogfood its existing rules.

## Falsifiers

- An independently reproduced case needs recursive authority or lifecycle
  semantics that cannot be represented by KFD-1/KFD-2/KFD-5/KFD-11.
- Deleting the Candidate changes a bounded governance decision despite all
  existing roots and receipts remaining fixed.
- Fusing the Candidate into existing KFDs creates an unavoidable ambiguity or
  grants an authority those KFDs cannot safely represent.
- A non-KFD adopter requires the same new object and cannot implement the
  closure without repeatedly reconstructing it.
- The fixed package cannot reject substituted roots, predecessors, authority,
  review, or claim overreach without extending its normative contract.

## Provisional reading

The current analytic result favors derivability and eventual `no-new-kfd`, but
this document has no disposition authority. Independent review may qualify,
retain, reject, or confirm the no-new result against the exact evidence cut.
