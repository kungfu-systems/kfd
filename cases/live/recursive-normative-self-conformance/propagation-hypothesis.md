# Propagation hypothesis

The procedure may transfer to standards, policy repositories, schema
registries, and governance systems that impose conformance on adopters while
changing their own rules. Transfer would require a non-KFD implementation to
retain exact normative cuts, independent verification, review and decision
authority, non-retroactivity, and a visible non-promotion outcome.

No cross-domain adopter is retained in this cut. Transfer therefore remains a
falsifiable hypothesis and cannot support qualification by itself.
