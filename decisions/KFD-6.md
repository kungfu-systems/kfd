# KFD-6: Autonomous discovery must remain grounded in causal experience

- Status: draft
- Number: 6
- Kind: procedure
- Applies to: every kungfu-systems autonomous or agent-operated system that proposes primitives, models, policies, or contracts from episode, event, trace, or experience corpora

## One sentence

Autonomous discovery must remain grounded in causal experience.

An autonomous loop compares candidates generated by multiple declared methods
without replacing reality with its narrative or certifying itself.

## Status and gate

KFD-6 is a draft procedure derived from KFD-1 through KFD-5. It defines an
experiment and activation gate; it does not claim that current agents already
satisfy it.

It applies when a system claims it can discover a load-bearing primitive or
equivalent authority-bearing model with less direct human translation than
KFD-5 assumes. Retrieval, summarization, clustering, anomaly detection,
candidate generation, or perspective simulation that makes no promotion claim
does not require KFD-6.

## Causal experience

Causal experience is an inspectable projection of contact with reality:
observations, choices, actions, dependencies, outcomes, costs, failures,
retries, overrides, and receipts connected by causal links.
It must remain capable of contradicting the loop's current model.

An Episode is a useful carrier, not reality itself. The loop declares its
corpus and immutable evidence cut, observer and capture boundary, accepted and
excluded sources, gaps, degradation, bias, and consequences represented only
by proxies.

## Experiment procedure

KFD has no evidence that one genesis method dominates. Method choice therefore
remains experimental.

An autonomous loop:

1. binds an immutable causal-experience cut, observer, capture boundary, gaps,
   and biases;
2. declares the current ontology and observation perspective;
3. declares generation methods, model classes, procedures, resource budgets,
   and disconfirming tests;
4. runs applicable perspective, anomaly, reconstruction, causal-variable,
   compression, or hybrid experiments;
5. compares methods over shared evidence, resource budgets, and metrics,
   including candidate yield, false-candidate rate, ontology distance,
   compression, intervention, transfer, falsifiability, cost, and auditability;
6. compares against fixed-ontology and no-new-primitive baselines;
7. proposes candidate identity, boundary, authority, lifecycle, and operations,
   then hands the candidate to KFD-5;
8. separates discovery evidence from held-out evaluation and promotion
   authority, then replays historical cases, counterfactuals, and disconfirming
   experience;
9. runs shadow evaluation before bounded intervention;
10. collects new causal experience and records rejection, revision,
    provisional retention, or a promotion request.

Each experiment preserves its method, perspective, ontology, causal basis,
procedure, evidence cut, budget, result, and disconfirming evidence. Perspective
methods additionally preserve source views, transformed view, reconstruction
policy, loss, and degradation.

Flattening Episodes into anonymous tokens or events hides their observation and
consequence boundaries. Better prediction, retrieval, clustering, compression,
or causal fit is not by itself evidence of primitive discovery.

## Optional boundary hypothesis

When a candidate appears at a contact surface, the loop may test prior implicit
coordination, changed pressure, recurring failures, mediation, and narrower
internal alternatives. Boundary pressure is useful when present, not mandatory
for every method.

## Authority and self-certification

The generator must not be the sole verifier, and generated examples cannot be
the only evidence. Evaluation includes an independent evidence cut, evaluator,
verifier, policy, or accountable participant whose failure is not identical to
the generator's.

Discovery and promotion authority remain separate. Humans may continue to own
value, safety, legal, allocation, or irreversible decisions. Autonomous genesis
does not grant authority to change those boundaries.

## Activation

KFD-6 remains draft until a real implementation:

- compares multiple generation methods and required baselines under bounded
  conditions;
- produces candidates that survive KFD-5 qualification;
- rejects attractive false candidates and method artifacts;
- preserves observer, provenance, causal, responsibility, and degraded-state
  boundaries;
- avoids self-certification;
- transfers at least one candidate across contexts without hiding local facts;
- leaves an auditable path from experience cut through experiment,
  qualification, and promotion decision.

Failure is evidence against activation, not a reason to weaken the gate.

## Relation to KFD-1 through KFD-5

KFD-1 protects experience and contracts. KFD-2 binds trust to facts and risk.
KFD-3 protects participant choice. KFD-4 declares and transforms perspectives.
KFD-5 separates genesis from qualification. KFD-6 attempts to internalize and
compare that complete motion:

```text
Atlas_n -> action -> Episodes -> generation experiments
        -> KFD-5 qualification -> candidate Atlas_n+1
```
