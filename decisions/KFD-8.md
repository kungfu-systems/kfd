# KFD-8: Perspective must remain bound to admitted facts and declared loss

- Status: draft
- Number: 8
- Kind: principle
- Applies to: any adopting product, repository, agent workflow, or participant-facing interface that carries a situated view into consequential action

## One sentence

Perspective must remain bound to admitted facts, source, cut, scope, and
declared loss.

## Rule

An **Atlas** is the independently addressable perspective coordinate used to
judge action. It binds at least:

- the observer or participant position;
- accepted fact sources and their authority;
- the exact Fact cut used for judgment;
- scope, relevance, and projection boundaries;
- freshness, known omissions, unresolved conflict, and declared loss.

An Atlas is not complete reality, generic context, a retrieval result, a
database snapshot, or a world model by definition. Those may implement or
contribute to an Atlas only when the decision-relevant source and boundary
remain inspectable.

## Independence

Atlas answers:

```text
From where, from which admitted facts, and at which cut is this action judged?
```

It does not supply continuing direction or authority. One Pursuit may be
judged through several Atlases; one Atlas may support several Pursuits; a
Warrant may require an exact Atlas root without becoming that Atlas.

Refreshing, transforming, degrading, comparing, or superseding an Atlas must
preserve prior roots and declare the transformation and its loss. A current
reference may move; an earlier perspective must not be silently rewritten.

## Gate

A conforming claim:

- gives the Atlas a stable identity and immutable version or Fact-cut binding;
- keeps source authority distinct from relevance or retrieval rank;
- makes stale, degraded, conflicted, omitted, or transformed state visible;
- preserves perspective lineage across replay and comparison;
- rejects inference of Pursuit, Warrant, completeness, or current validity
  from visible payload alone;
- allows a Domain Profile to choose storage, fields, lifecycle vocabulary, and
  presentation without changing the coordinate's responsibility.

## Status boundary

KFD-8 is a numbered draft promoted from the
[Atlas action perspective candidate](../drafts/atlas-action-perspective.md).
The candidate remains public source lineage. This decision allocates the
number and canonical term, but it does not claim universal empirical
qualification or active status.

## Relationship

KFD-7 establishes the Action Responsibility Geometry. KFD-8 specifies its
perspective coordinate. KFD-4 supplies the underlying rule that views remain
bound to declared perspectives; KFD-1 through KFD-3 supply the fact, trust,
and cooperation foundation required to transfer or replay an Atlas.

## Non-claims

KFD-8 does not require one serialization, store, API, screen, retrieval
system, or product name. It does not establish a God's-eye state or require
every low-consequence action to expose an explicit Atlas object.
