# KFD-9: Continuing direction must outlive the actions that advance it

- Status: draft
- Number: 9
- Kind: principle
- Applies to: any adopting product, repository, agent workflow, or participant-facing interface that preserves intent across actions, participants, tools, or revisions

## One sentence

Continuing direction must preserve identity, progress meaning, lineage, and
settlement independently of the actions that advance it.

## Rule

A **Pursuit** is the independently addressable direction coordinate for an
intended real-world change. It preserves:

- durable identity across Episodes, sessions, participants, tools, and plans;
- the intended change or maintained condition and the relation by which
  consequence counts as progress;
- decomposition, dependency, contribution, revision, and successor relations;
- settlement semantics covering completion, continuation, pause, abandonment,
  and unresolved state without requiring one universal terminal success;
- the distinction between prospective relevance and realized consequence.

A Pursuit is not a session, one-shot task, mutable prose body, plan, command,
or Episode. Those may project, decompose, or advance it without becoming its
identity.

## Independence

Pursuit answers:

```text
What intended change continues across this action, and what consequence would
matter to it?
```

It does not grant authority or define the perspective from which progress is
judged. Decomposition does not transfer Warrant, Atlas, trust, completion, or
settlement. Technical success, progress, completion, and settlement remain
distinct claims.

A material change to direction or success meaning must remain visible as a
revision, fork, successor, or supersession rather than silent replacement.

## Gate

A conforming claim:

- preserves Pursuit identity across non-isomorphic actions and handoffs;
- uses typed relations rather than treating every relation as parent-child
  inheritance;
- keeps prospective relevance distinct from observed progress and settlement;
- prevents child completion or Episode sealing from silently settling a
  parent;
- preserves revision and successor lineage;
- allows Domain Profiles to choose hierarchy, lifecycle vocabulary, success
  policy, and interface while retaining the coordinate's responsibility.

## Status boundary

KFD-9 is a numbered draft promoted from the
[Pursuit intent continuity candidate](../drafts/pursuit-intent-continuity.md).
The candidate remains public source lineage. This decision allocates the
number and canonical term, but it does not claim universal empirical
qualification or active status.

## Relationship

KFD-7 establishes the Action Responsibility Geometry. KFD-9 specifies its
direction coordinate. KFD-8 provides the perspective through which progress
may be judged; KFD-10 provides authority for bounded transitions; Episode
preserves what actually occurred.

## Non-claims

KFD-9 does not require one task tree, workflow, project system, storage layout,
or user-facing object. It does not make every local goal durable or prescribe
one domain's definition of value.
