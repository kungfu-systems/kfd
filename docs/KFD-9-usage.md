KFD-9 is a numbered draft. It promotes Pursuit from an elaboration candidate
to the canonical continuing-direction coordinate.

## Adoption shape

An adopter should preserve an immutable Pursuit version plus lineage across
revision, decomposition, fork, pause, settlement, abandonment, and
supersession. Relation types remain explicit; a generic parent-child edge must
not silently carry authority, perspective, completion, or trust.

The Domain Profile owns concrete success policy, hierarchy, lifecycle terms,
storage, and presentation. A simple local goal may project a Pursuit while the
work remains session-compressible.

## Qualification

Retain evidence that:

- one direction survives several Episodes, tools, sessions, or participants;
- plan revision differs from direction revision, fork, and successor;
- prospective relevance, technical success, progress, completion, and
  settlement can vary independently;
- child progress does not silently settle a parent;
- the independent Pursuit becomes visible only at the declared complexity
  breakpoint.

## Migration from the candidate

The numbered decision and formal reference now own the draft rule. The
candidate remains public genesis and qualification lineage.
