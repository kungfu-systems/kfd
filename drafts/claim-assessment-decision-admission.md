# Claim, Assessment, Decision, and Admission

- Status: promoted
- Kind candidate: procedure
- Promoted to: KFD-11
- Source: Agent-generated development ontology and later cross-lineage compression, grounded in Fact Admission, KFD-2 trust semantics, KFD-10 authority allocation, Mission/Go implementation, and Action Loop settlement

## Candidate

Consequential settlement must preserve the difference between an exact Claim,
a purpose-bound Assessment, an authorized Decision, and the independently
recorded result of Admission.

## Development origin

Agents first generated Claim and purpose-bound Assessment while designing trust
execution, then composed Claim, Assessment, and Decision with Mission/Go during
Kungfu development. Fact Admission, Project Cut review, and recoverable Action
Loop settlement later exposed that a valid Decision still does not prove that
the owning authority admitted a successor state.

Replaying those development lines exposed one portable boundary: evidence does
not state a proposition, assessment does not grant authority, a decision does
not apply itself, and an attempted effect does not prove successor state. The
later KFD allocation discussion recognized and compressed the existing model;
it did not supply the intermediate objects.

## Promotion boundary

KFD-11 allocates the procedure as a numbered draft. Promotion does not activate
the decision, prove universal minimality, require four physical records, or
replace KFD-2 Claim and Assessment schemas.
